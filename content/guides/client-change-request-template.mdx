## Copy-and-paste change request template

Replace the bracketed details, remove fields that do not apply, and keep the language specific enough that both sides know exactly what will happen after approval.

<MdxTemplate>{`Project: [Project name]
Client: [Client name]
Change request: [Short descriptive title]

Requested change
[Describe the additional work in plain language.]

Original scope reference
[State what the original agreement included and why this request is additional.]

Deliverables included in this change
- [Deliverable one]
- [Deliverable two]
- [Any exclusions or assumptions]

Additional price
[Amount and currency, plus applicable tax or payment terms]

Schedule impact
[No change / adds X business days / revised delivery date]

Approval
Please approve this change before work begins. Approval confirms the additional work, price, and schedule impact described above.

Decision: [Approved / Changes requested]
Client name: [Name]
Decision date: [Date]`}</MdxTemplate>

## How to price the additional work

Use the same pricing method you use for normal project work, then account for coordination, testing, revisions, and schedule disruption—not only the visible production task.

### Fixed price

Best when the added deliverables and revision allowance can be defined clearly.

### Hourly estimate

Useful when the requirement is uncertain. State the estimated range, rate, and approval needed before exceeding it.

### Rush fee

Appropriate when the requested deadline requires rescheduling other committed work.
</MdxSection>

<MdxSection>
## Common mistakes to avoid

- **Starting before approval:** urgency can turn an unapproved request into unpaid work.
- **Using vague descriptions:** “website updates” does not define what is included or excluded.
- **Showing only the price:** include delivery impact and assumptions so the full tradeoff is visible.
- **Sounding accusatory:** describe the difference from the original scope calmly and factually.
- **Keeping approval in scattered messages:** store the request and decision together.
</MdxSection>
