# Brag Plan: ScopeYes

## What is this app?
ScopeYes is client approval and scope change software for freelancers and small teams: it documents extra work, its cost and timeline impact, and the client decision before work begins.

## The angle
Turn the awkward, scattered scope-creep conversation into one calm, visible decision. The film starts with the project's bluntest line, then follows a real change request from description to impact to client approval, treating clarity as the premium feature.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
“Stop doing extra work for free.” arrives as a decisive editorial statement. “extra work” carries the project's Instrument Serif-style emphasis while a change-request card rises into view at the edge of the frame.

## Key moments (the middle)

- A new change request fills in with the real project example: “Analytics dashboard,” “$750,” and “5 business days.”
- The focused client review shows the requested work, cost, timeline, and one clear “Approve request” action.
- The approval action resolves into the real product state: “Approved — ready to move forward.”

## Outro / punchline
“A clear decision before work begins.” resolves into the ScopeYes mark and the line “Control scope creep.”

## User flow worth showing
Describe the change → add price and timeline impact → send a private review link → client approves and the decision is recorded.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet premium product film for a calm, professional boundary-setting tool
- Interpretation: confident editorial pacing, generous holds, exact UI motion, and restrained sound; no hype language or frantic transitions.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: `#f8f8f4`
- Accent: `#176b55`
- Text: `#1b1c18`
- Display font: Instrument Serif for emphasized editorial words; Inter for product and headline structure
- Body font: Inter
- Strongest visual element: the white change-request review card with an “Awaiting approval” status, $750 cost, +5 day timeline, and approval controls

## Share copy (draft)
Made ScopeYes so “quick little changes” stop turning into free work. Scope, price, timeline, and one clear client decision — before the work begins.

## Audio direction
- Role: sparse professional accents over a steady, warm business bed
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.mp3`
- Music treatment: begin at 0 with a 0.6s fade-in, stay low at roughly 0.30–0.34, and fade out from 18.56s to 20s
- Music cue guidance: bundled preset at `assets/music/cues/happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12-by-ende-dot-app.music-cues.json`; estimated 109.96 BPM; major cue locks at 8.74s, 13.11s, and 17.47s; form-field sequence may use every other beat around 4.39s, 5.34s, 6.56s, and 7.64s so text remains readable
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; use per-frame RMS/bass to let the background warmth and card shadow breathe by only a few percent. No waveform, equalizer, strobe, or text pulsing.
- SFX posture: sparse; one soft hook/reveal impact, one clean approval click, and one warm brand-close accent
- Audio-coupled moments: hook settles at 0.56s; form fields resolve on widely spaced beat points; approval press at 13.11s; final mark at 17.47s
- Restraint rule: the soundtrack supports the product demonstration and never competes with the readable UI.
